# What we adapted from repair marketplaces

13 October 2026. Read public service pages, not private accounts or sales ledgers. Offers are supply evidence; we did not establish their conversion or income. Wording below is our synthesis; no competitor text, testimonials or portfolio was copied into our offer.

| Source | Observed pattern | Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| [Upwork Python script service](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/development-it-your-python-script-fixed-or-written-with-tests-you-can-run-2107801264102420660) | $30/$75/$150 tiers; reproduce/fix/verify flow; message or purchase buttons; seller claims public merged PR evidence | Smaller starting scope and inspectable work, without adopting the seller's claimed track record |
| [LaborX one API bug](https://laborx.com/gigs/i-will-fix-one-python-api-bug-and-add-a-regression-test-122277) | 50 USDT, message before ordering, patch/test/instructions, scope exclusions | Explicit deliverables and fit check; visible profile had no review evidence for this offer |
| [Fiverr small Python fix](https://www.fiverr.com/farhan_ali_256/python-bug-fixing-debugging-and-custom-python-scripts) | $5 basic, bounded code size, defined delivery/revision, contact and checkout controls | Clear entry package and limits; not the $5 price or satisfaction claims, which lack our cost/quality validation |

Dollar display prices, USDT and USDC are different billing contexts; do not average them or infer an optimal price. Deliverables, fees, automation and review differ. A cheaper service may be suitable or may hide a narrower scope; the page alone cannot settle that.

Our changes: error + expected result as initial intake instead of nine fields; 30-USDC tiny offline-script quote invitation with tight eligibility, alongside the existing around-75 integration scope; concrete proof link; direct call to action above network descriptions. No guaranteed completion window before availability confirmation. The USDC/account requirement remains a possible barrier: it is not solved by better wording. No card checkout or Upwork/Fiverr seller account was created. Existing discussions are not service orders.

Test the revision using qualified inquiries, reproducer submissions, feasible quotes, orders, accepted delivery and attributed receipts. Preserve the earlier version and avoid treating no response as a price rejection. No conversion uplift or sale demonstrated yet.
