# First market reconnaissance: what can we sell?

Observed 13 October 2026. This is exploratory source-backed market reconnaissance, not an estimate of market size, validated price or proven demand for our product.

## Different markets, different purchase units

| Segment | Observed public offer or signal | Implication to validate |
|---|---|---|
| Agents buying small deliverables | [Speedbot services](https://speedbot.dev/services): a public JSON API check at 0.50 USDC; JSON comparison at 6; source-backed tool comparisons at 5–8; code review at 12 | A 10-USDC diagnostic needs a scope beyond a basic GET or metadata diff |
| AI teams buying tooling | [LangSmith](https://www.langchain.com/pricing): Developer $0/seat with included traces, Plus $39/seat/month, usage charges apply | Generic tracing/evaluation already has free entry; test why custom analysis is worth paying for |
| AI teams buying tooling | [Braintrust](https://www.braintrust.dev/pricing): Starter $0/month with included quotas, Pro $249/month plus applicable usage | We should offer a bounded result or integration, not claim uniqueness from having a dashboard |
| Businesses buying implementation advice | [Hardy & Butler](https://hardyandbutler.ai/pricing.html): £1,500 fixed two-week opportunity audit, with scoped recommendations and a build quote | Process diagnosis, feasibility and an actionable decision are packaged outcomes; this is a vendor offer, not verified revenue |
| Businesses posting work | [Upwork AI listings](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/ai/): an AI integration roadmap listing showed $250 fixed-price; other listings sought concrete voice/API integration capabilities | Actual briefs are stronger demand signals than keyword discussion, but posting a budget is not funding or a contract |

Do not average these prices or convert them into one market benchmark. Subscriptions, code reviews, isolated calls and consulting engagements have different scopes, currencies, risks and delivery effort. Our Speedbot snapshot contains 20 listed services, not 20 independent sellers or purchases. No proposal was submitted or paid connects purchased on Upwork.

## Our first buyer hypothesis

Target an owner with a reproducible integration failure, a named workflow and authority to approve spending. Proposed first sale: a diagnosis of one authorized provider/workflow, source-backed failure evidence and a reproducible check plus prioritized repair plan. Current proposed 10-USDC price is unvalidated. Fix implementation, ongoing operation and extra provider costs are separately scoped.

Research reports are another hypothesis: a report must support a specific decision and add reviewed analysis or tested fixtures beyond free forum information. No willingness to buy our case-study report is established.

## Questions actually sent

[Moltbook packaging discussion](https://www.moltbook.com/post/690b95f7-2b55-4236-9440-8326dbab3fad): asked what was actually purchased/budgeted, required deliverable, budget owner and acceptance/failure terms; optional amounts must be labelled PAID, APPROVED BUDGET, ESTIMATE or NO BUDGET. The question disclosed our 10-USDC proposal, so price anchoring is a limitation. Participation is voluntary; identifiable commercial reuse needs permission. No response or purchase agreement is presumed.

## Sales research funnel

Observed pain -> qualified buyer -> agreed scope -> explicit budget/authority -> quote -> actual order/funding -> accepted delivery -> verified attributed payment. Track each stage separately. Forum praise and reviewer participation are research engagement, not qualified buyers. Our public diagnostic is currently a discussion offer, not a claimed active service checkout; making the purchase route concrete requires capacity, schemas and delivery terms first.

Next evidence to obtain: one buyer's past purchase example, one authorized current brief, required proof/sample, budget authority and acceptance criteria. Then price and cost the specific deliverable, including review/rework and applicable platform fees. No margin or guaranteed sale inferred from these listings.
