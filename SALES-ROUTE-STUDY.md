# Choose one deliverable before scaling the Verbund

13 October 2026. Decision: which narrow service should be tested for a first sale with zero additional upfront spending and supervised delivery? Unit: one fixed-scope customer deliverable. This is a purposive, exploratory comparison of six offers, not a representative market study, proven sales ranking or conversion prediction.

## Transparent priority rubric

Each criterion is an analyst-coded ordinal 1–5: 1 weak, 3 mixed, 5 strong. Demand evidence weight 25; our supervised delivery fit 25; clarity of acceptance 20; plausible unit economics 15; accessible buying route 15. Score = sum(weight × rating)/5. Ratings are judgments grounded below, not measured quantities. A 75 does NOT mean 75% chance of sale. No offer gets a 5 for demand because there are no verified sales of our offer.

| Candidate | Demand | Delivery | Acceptance | Economics | Buying route | Priority /100 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| One Python/API bug fixed + regression test | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 75 |
| One AI-media revision plan | 2 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 61 |
| Final AI voiceover + pronunciation QA | 4 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 58 |
| AI-efficiency research report | 1 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 48 |
| Finished AI product ad | 3 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 44 |
| Repair an AI-generated mesh | 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 39 |

Rationale: API repair combines a buyer asking for a fix with our Python/testing capacity and a reproducible pass/fail artifact. Delivery is 4, not 5: feasibility, access and manual review remain necessary. Media planning is deliverable but direct willingness to buy it is unverified. Voiceover has a contracting signal but lacks our demonstrated voice/prosody review capacity. Ads/meshes require specialist portfolios, tools and output QA not demonstrated here. Reports are easy to create but their purchase decision and incremental value are unclear. Buying routes score low because reaching budget owners and completing a service order have not been established; our existing downloadable audit checkout is a different offer.

Reproducible inputs: [JSON](sales-route-scores.json), [notebook](sales-route-analysis.ipynb). Calculations were executed and checked against an independently hand-computed API score: (100+100+100+45+30)/5 = 75. Alternative demand-heavy weights (40/15/20/10/15) keep API repair first, put voiceover second; delivery-heavy weights (15/40/20/10/15) keep API repair first, media planning second. This is limited sensitivity testing, not statistical confidence. Rating errors and missing buyers can change the choice.

## Recommended single route

**Target:** owner of a small Python-based AI integration with one reproducible failure. Media API integrations are a relevant subset; the broad bug-fix evidence does not prove that subset buys from us.

**Result:** one agreed bug repaired, regression test showing before/after behavior, and a short root-cause/handover note. Scope excludes redesign, multiple unrelated faults and live deployment. Access must be authorized; sanitized reproducer or repository branch preferred. A paid diagnostic-only phase requires its own explicit quote, not silently substituting a report for a promised fix.

**Price to test:** quote invitation around 75 USDC for a clearly small, feasible bug, with final quote only after reproducer review. This is neither a validated price nor a published purchasable service. The observed $50 broad brief is a demand anchor, not a price recommendation or currency conversion. At assumed 30 USDC/hour labor, 5% seller-side fees and 30% contribution target, 75 permits 97.5 minutes total work if other variable costs are zero. Actual fees, buyer fees, review/rework and dependencies need checking. Reject or separately quote larger tasks before acceptance. No guarantee that every bug fits.

**One order route to establish:** a fixed-scope service order on Speedbot after input eligibility and capacity confirmation, with delivery/acceptance/payment terms. Current public discussion is NOT that order route. Human buyers may resist USDC or lack an account; interview explicitly about that friction. Do not move clients off a marketplace against its rules. No funded order, payment request or financial commitment was created in this study.

## Validation and score updates

Run a bounded first cohort of 10 qualified problem owners as an operational test, not a statistically adequate sample. Qualify actual issue, ownership, urgency and budget authority; review a safe reproducer; send a scope-specific quote only when feasible. Track delivered outreach, substantive reply, qualified need, quote, funded order, accepted work, verified attributed receipt, review/rework time and contribution separately. Deduplicate organizations/owners and separate researcher interest from buyers. Do not treat unanswered messages as price objections.

Decision rule: a real paid/accepted case supports continuing but not scaling globally. If at least three qualified owners provide reproducible problems yet no feasible quote is accepted, investigate their stated objections, payment friction and alternative providers before more cold outreach. If no qualified problems emerge, revise channel or segment before building more product. These are operational triggers, not estimates of statistical significance. Update scores only with dated evidence and retain rejected offers/negative findings. The actual target metric is verified contribution per delivered customer outcome, not outreach volume or number of studies.
