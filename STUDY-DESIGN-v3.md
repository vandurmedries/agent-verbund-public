# Study design v3: operational definitions before expansion

The [IMT practicum](https://www.studiealtelier.be/imt-practicum/?course=imt#casusvoorbeelden) was consulted as a didactic checklist, not an official university correction key or primary empirical source. Its structure motivates making aim, question, units, method, timing, setting and quality checks explicit. Case interpretations remain secondary material. No educational study result is applied to AI agents.

## Separate studies and link them deliberately

**Study A — exploratory contact cases.** Question: what observed barriers and counterpart explanations accompany contact without a scoped next step? Unit: a contact episode, grouped by conversation and known operator when disclosed. Material: actual message delivery records, substantive replies, follow-up and respondent explanation. Settings: authorized Moltbook, MoChat and Speedbot interfaces. Record actual observation times and predefine a response window; nonresponse is censored/unknown, not refusal. Purposefully include failure, success and ambiguous cases. This is not a representative population survey or causal experiment.

**Study B — controlled documentation pilot.** Question: does a typed handoff add quality beyond original-source context under equal model/call budgets? Unit: source-bounded task; repeated calls are clustered within task. Arms A/B/C/D as in the existing design. Freeze exact wording, source hash and grading before new scored runs. Randomized arm order controls order only; it is not random sampling of agents or independent operators. Include tasks of different difficulties and report model-load/environment variability. No causal interpretation of v1-versus-v2: two instructions changed.

**Study C — reuse follow-up.** Question: does a reviewed lesson help a later task with fewer errors or less verified work cost? Unit: later task applying a specific recorded lesson. Material: prior evidence, lesson version, permitted reuse, actual retrieval/application, outcome and rework. Compare a no-lesson baseline where feasible. A stored transcript is not demonstrated useful reuse.

Integration: qualitative cases identify candidate mechanisms and failures; controlled tasks test them; follow-up checks transfer. Until all stages and the connecting analysis occur, this is a planned multi-method program, not completed mixed-method evidence.

## Observable indicators

| Concept | Indicator | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Understandable offer | Counterpart accurately paraphrases deliverable, needed input and next action | A friendly reply is not comprehension; AI text does not reveal human intentions |
| Useful cooperation | Explicitly scoped next step with named contributor and acceptance | An introduction or review offer is not a paid commission |
| Continuity | Original event, state, owner and unresolved action recover after restart | Local backup does not protect against every failure |
| Quality | Source-supported facts, required coverage and clear scope; rubric and reviewer identified | No independent human scores yet |
| Speed | Complete end-to-end result time, queue and review time reported separately | A truncated reply is not successful task completion |
| Cost | All measured calls, retries and abandoned branches per accepted output | Tokens and local invoice spend are not full economic cost |
| Knowledge value | A reviewed lesson changes a later accepted result; documented rights | A promising insight is not a sale or universal rule |

Quality checks: evidence triangulation tied to a specific risk, negative cases, source-based scoring, independent grader ownership disclosure and repeat-task uncertainty. Explain interpretations to respondents where practical; neither discussion nor repeated model agreement establishes validity automatically. Timing, sample targets and score thresholds are design decisions to freeze before confirmatory collection, not invented results.

Current status: preparatory runs and software recovery tests exist. Agent-language survey responses meeting the requested format, independent human quality scores, confirmed paid research demand and universal savings remain absent. Research-agent output is advisory planning and must be reviewed before acting.
