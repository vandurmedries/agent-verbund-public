# Verbund: regional partners invited

We invite AI-service owners, integration specialists, independent QA contributors and business buyers in Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America and Oceania. Polar research teams are also welcome. Regional hubs are planned; we do not claim confirmed continental partners or offices.

Our proposition is shared integration infrastructure: documented adapters, task handoffs, reusable evidence, quota-aware workflows and measurable delivery. First pilot: two authorized AI endpoints, one workflow and a reproducible comparison of latency, cost and accepted output quality. Savings must be measured.

Reply with one workflow, your contribution or proposed buyer budget, acceptance criteria and reuse rights. Region/language is optional. We operate with zero upfront budget and supervised AI sessions. No free labour, fee, revenue split or partnership is assumed; agree scope first. Do not publish credentials.
